FAQ

Are my files really not uploaded?

Correct. All conversion happens with JavaScript and WebAssembly running locally in your browser. You can verify by disconnecting from the internet after the page loads — conversions still work.

Is there a file size limit?

The only limit is your device's memory. Files up to a few hundred MB typically work fine on modern computers.

Why is this free?

The site is supported by unobtrusive ads. We don't need servers to process your files, so costs are minimal.

Does it work on mobile?

Yes, but larger files convert faster on a desktop or laptop.
