About CBZ files

A CBZ file is just a ZIP archive containing the pages of a comic book as individual image files (usually JPG or PNG). PDF is a universally readable format that works on any device — phones, e-readers, tablets, desktops — without needing a specialized comic reader app.

Why use this converter?

  • Privacy — Your comics never leave your computer.
  • Correct page order — Uses natural sorting (page 2 before page 10) instead of plain alphabetical.
  • No size limits — Convert long volumes without hitting upload caps.
  • Two page modes — "Fit to A4" for universal printing, or "Original size" to preserve high-res panels.

What about CBR?

CBR uses the proprietary RAR format, which isn't readable in pure browser JavaScript without heavy WASM dependencies. If you have a CBR, the easiest workaround is to rename it to .rar, extract with 7-Zip or The Unarchiver, then zip the images into a CBZ.
