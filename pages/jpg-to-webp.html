About JPG and WEBP

Need to send a photo, post it online, or open it in an app that doesn't accept JPG? Converting to WEBP takes one click here. Multiple files are returned as a single ZIP.

What this converter does

  • Decodes your JPG files using your browser's built-in image engine.
  • Re-encodes each image as WEBP.
  • For a single file, you get the converted image directly. For multiple files, you get a ZIP.

Is the conversion lossy?

WEBP at the same visual quality is typically 25-35% smaller than JPG and 50% smaller than PNG. The quality control above adjusts the trade-off; 0.85 (Standard) is a good default.

Privacy

Conversion happens entirely in your browser. Nothing is uploaded. You can verify by disconnecting from the internet after the page loads — this page will still convert.
