How does PDF to EPUB work?

PDFs are fixed-layout — every character has a precise position on a fixed-size page. EPUBs are reflowable — text adapts to the screen, font size, and orientation of whatever device opens it. Converting between them is fundamentally a text-extraction problem.

What gets preserved?

  • All text content, in reading order.
  • Paragraph breaks based on line spacing.
  • Chapter structure when headings are detected (e.g., "Chapter 1").
  • Title and author metadata from the PDF.

What doesn't transfer well?

  • Complex layouts (multi-column scientific papers can produce mixed text order).
  • Scanned PDFs without OCR — there's no text to extract.
  • Footnotes, page numbers, and headers may appear inline.

For best results, start with a text-based PDF (not a scan).
