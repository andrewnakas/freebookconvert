Why combine images into a PDF?

PDF is the universal format for documents. Combining a stack of receipts, screenshots, scanned pages, or photos into one PDF means a single file to email, archive, or print — instead of a folder of loose images.

Features

  • Any quantity — combine 2 or 200 images.
  • Mixed types — JPG, PNG, and WEBP can be combined in the same PDF.
  • Preserves order — pages appear in the order you added them. Use the × button to remove and re-add to reorder.
  • A4-sized pages — images are scaled to fit A4 with aspect ratio preserved.
  • Private — nothing is uploaded.

Tips

For scanned documents, use the "High" quality setting. For photos shared online or by email, "Standard" or "Compact" produces a much smaller file with no visible difference.
