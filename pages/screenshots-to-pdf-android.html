Combine Android Screenshots Into One PDF

You captured 15 screenshots of a chat thread, a recipe, or a bank statement. Now you need them as one PDF for email or storage. Drop them here and the browser builds a single PDF in page order. No Play Store app, no upload, no ads in the middle of the flow.

or drag from Files

PNG, JPG, WEBP. Pick them in the order you want pages.

Why not just use the Files app "Save as PDF"?

One UI, MIUI, and Pixel's Files app all have a Print to PDF route, but they save one screenshot at a time, or they reorder by file modification time, or they downsample for print. None of them let you say "this screenshot is page 1, that one is page 2." This page does.

Works on every Android skin

  • Samsung Galaxy: Samsung Internet and Chrome both work. Drag from One UI split view.
  • Google Pixel: Chrome handles it natively. Files app screenshots load instantly.
  • OnePlus / Oppo: Browser on OxygenOS / ColorOS works the same.
  • Xiaomi / Redmi: MIUI's Mi Browser, plus Chrome, both supported.

Long screenshots (scrolling capture)

If your screenshot is a "Scroll Capture" or "Long Screenshot" (Samsung), it's still a PNG and works here. The PDF page will be tall but the resolution is preserved. If the screenshot is enormous, set Quality to Compact to keep the PDF under your email size limit.

Reordering on the fly

Tap the × next to any screenshot to remove it. Re-pick it to push it to the end. Pages match the order you added them.

Mobile data off? Still works.

Turn off Wi-Fi and mobile data after the page loads. The conversion still runs. The PDF still downloads. That's because nothing leaves the device — the browser does the work locally with pdf-lib.

WhatsApp / Drive / Gmail handoff

The finished PDF lands in your Downloads folder. From the notification, tap Share to pipe it into WhatsApp, Drive, Gmail, or any app that takes a PDF.

Combine Android screenshots into one PDF

  1. Open in Chrome. Open this page in Chrome (or any Android browser). No app install needed.
  2. Tap the drop zone. Tap the drop zone, choose your gallery, and select the screenshots in the order you want them as pages.
  3. Combine. Tap Combine into PDF. The PDF is built in the browser tab.
  4. Save. The PDF lands in your Downloads folder. Share it from there to Drive, Gmail, or WhatsApp.

Questions people actually ask

Do I need to install an app from the Play Store?

No. This is a web page. It works in Chrome, Samsung Internet, Brave, and Firefox on Android with no install.

What about the built-in Files app PDF option?

Some Android skins (Samsung One UI, MIUI) have a Save as PDF option in the Files app share sheet, but it builds one PDF per file and doesn't merge a batch in a chosen order. This page does the merge.

Will this work on a Samsung Galaxy?

Yes. Samsung Internet, Chrome, and Edge on Galaxy phones all work. You can also drag screenshots from One UI 6 split view straight into the drop zone.

Are my screenshots uploaded anywhere?

No. The PDF is built locally in Chrome using pdf-lib. Turn off mobile data after this page loads and the conversion still runs.
