Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-05-14

Your files

The files you convert on FreeBookConvert are never uploaded to our servers. All processing happens inside your browser using JavaScript and WebAssembly. We have no way to see, store, or share the content of the files you process.

Analytics

We use basic anonymized analytics (page views, country-level location, device type) to understand which converters are most useful. We do not track individuals across the internet or build advertising profiles.

Advertising

FreeBookConvert displays ads served by Google AdSense. Google may use cookies to serve ads based on your visits to this and other websites. You can opt out of personalized advertising at Google Ads Settings.

Third-party libraries

Some pages load JavaScript libraries from public CDNs (jsDelivr, unpkg, cdnjs). These CDNs may log standard HTTP request data (IP, user agent, timestamp). They do not receive the contents of your converted files.
