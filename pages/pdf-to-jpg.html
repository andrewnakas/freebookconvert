What this converter does

It uses Mozilla's pdf.js — the same PDF engine built into Firefox — to render each PDF page to a canvas, then exports the canvas as a JPG (or PNG). Nothing is uploaded.

When you'd use this

  • Posting individual pages on social media or in a forum.
  • Embedding a page of a PDF as an image in a document or slide.
  • Extracting figures or charts from a research paper.
  • Sharing one specific page without sending the whole PDF.

JPG or PNG?

JPG is best for photos and complex graphics; PNG is best for diagrams, screenshots, and pages with text or sharp edges. Files are typically larger as PNG but lossless.
