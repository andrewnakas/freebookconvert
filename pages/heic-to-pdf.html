Why convert HEIC to PDF?

iPhones save photos in HEIC by default. When you need to share a batch of photos with someone on Windows, attach them to an email, upload to a portal that doesn't accept HEIC, or print them as a single document, PDF is the universal solution.

What's the difference vs. other tools?

  • One step — most tools make you convert to JPG, then build a PDF separately.
  • Private — your photos never leave your device.
  • No file count limit — combine 5, 50, or 500 photos.
  • Preserves orientation — photos taken sideways stay right-side-up.

Tip: reorder photos

The PDF pages follow the order you added the photos. Add them in the order you want them to appear, or use the × button to remove and re-add in a different order.
