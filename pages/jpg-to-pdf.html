Why a form wants a PDF and not a JPG

A JPG is a single picture with no notion of a page. A PDF has page size, margins, and a fixed print layout, which is why upload forms, printers, and filing systems ask for one. Converting does not change what the image looks like — it wraps it in a page so the rest of the world can handle it predictably.

What happens to your photo

  • One image becomes one page. Twelve JPGs become a twelve-page PDF.
  • Aspect ratio is preserved. Each photo is scaled to fit an A4 page without stretching. Landscape shots get white space above and below.
  • EXIF rotation is respected. A photo taken sideways lands the right way up.
  • Order follows the order you added. Remove a file with the × and re-add it to move it to the end.

Choosing a quality setting

JPG is already a lossy format, and re-encoding it into the PDF costs a little more. High keeps the photo close to the original and is the right pick for anything that will be printed or read closely, like a scanned document. Standard is fine for email. Compact can cut the file to a fraction of the size and still look correct on a phone — the sensible choice when a portal caps uploads at 5 MB.

Phone photos of documents

Photographing a document instead of scanning it is normal now, and it works well enough if you get the basics right: flat surface, no shadow across the page, camera parallel to the paper rather than at an angle. Crop before converting if your phone did not do it for you. If you also need the words in the document to be selectable or searchable rather than just a picture, this is not the tool for that — run the result through Make a PDF searchable or pull the text out with Image to text.

Questions people actually ask

Are my photos uploaded?

No. The conversion runs in this browser tab. You can disconnect from the internet after the page loads and it still works, which is the simplest way to prove nothing is being sent anywhere.

Can I convert several JPGs into one PDF?

Yes, that is the default. Select as many as you like and they become one multi-page PDF, one photo per page, in the order you added them.

Why is my PDF so much larger than the JPGs?

The High quality setting embeds the images at close to full fidelity. Switch to Standard or Compact and the file usually shrinks dramatically with no visible difference on screen.

Will the text in my photo be searchable?

No. The photo goes in as a picture, so the words are pixels. Use Make a PDF searchable afterwards to add a hidden text layer via OCR.

My iPhone photos will not drop in. Why?

Recent iPhones save HEIC, not JPG. Use the HEIC to PDF converter, which accepts them directly.

Can I choose Letter instead of A4?

Not in this tool. Pages are A4. The two sizes differ by about 5 mm in width, and printers set to Letter shrink-to-fit without any visible loss.
