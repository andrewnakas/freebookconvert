Why convert EPUB to TXT?

  • Search the contents with any text editor or grep tool.
  • Feed the book into AI assistants that don't accept EPUB directly.
  • Reformat into your own outline or note-taking system.
  • Make ebooks accessible to text-to-speech readers that don't handle EPUB well.

What's preserved?

All text content, in reading order, with chapter headings marked. Formatting like bold, italics, fonts, and images is removed — that's the point of plain text.
